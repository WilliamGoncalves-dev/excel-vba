# Excel & VBA Automation

A collection of Excel and VBA projects focused on **business process automation, operational efficiency, data consolidation, and workflow standardization**.

The projects in this repository were developed to solve practical business problems, particularly in administrative, financial, billing, and operational environments.

Rather than treating Excel only as a spreadsheet tool, these projects explore how Excel and VBA can be used as a lightweight automation layer between business systems, structured data, and users.

---

## 🎯 Repository Goals

This repository documents practical solutions developed with:

* Microsoft Excel
* VBA (Visual Basic for Applications)
* Excel formulas and lookup functions
* Data consolidation
* Business rule automation
* Outlook automation
* Report generation
* Process standardization

The main goal is to demonstrate how spreadsheet-based solutions can reduce manual work and transform repetitive operational processes into structured workflows.

## 🔐 Data Privacy

The projects documented here are portfolio versions of solutions originally developed in professional environments.

For confidentiality reasons:

* Customer names are anonymized.
* Sensitive business information is removed or replaced.
* Real customer email addresses are not included.
* Internal company information is not disclosed.
* Example data may differ from the original operational dataset.

The purpose of this repository is to demonstrate **technical reasoning, automation design, and problem-solving**, rather than to reproduce confidential company data.

---

## 📈 Focus

The projects emphasize a simple principle:

> **Automate repetitive work while preserving the business logic behind the process.**

The solutions documented here demonstrate how relatively lightweight technologies can produce meaningful operational improvements when they are designed around the actual workflow and business rules.
